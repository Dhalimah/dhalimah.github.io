About

01

I am a geologist and GIS analyst. Whatever the field, my approach is the same: rigorous spatial methods and clear outputs that turn complex problems into decisions.

I hold an M.Tech in Geoinformation Technology (Distinction) from the Federal University of Technology, Akure, in collaboration with the African Regional Institute for Geospatial Information Science and Technology (AFRIGIST), and a B.Sc. in Geology and Mineral Science from Kwara State University.

I work as a GIS Technologist at the Institute of Agricultural Research and Training (IAR&T), Obafemi Awolowo University, where I lead spatial analysis for the Land and Water Resources Management Programme. My work spans mineral prospectivity mapping, soil and land suitability, flood risk, cartography and spatial health analytics.

Featured research

Geology · Remote Sensing

Orogenic Gold Mineralization

Moro area, Kwara State

Combined ASTER multispectral imagery, Sentinel-1 SAR, and aeromagnetic and aero-radiometric data to map hydrothermal alteration and the structural framework of gold mineralization in the Nigerian schist belt, then ranked prospective zones in a Fuzzy AHP mineral potential map over 300+ km².

Skills used: ENVI, PCI Geomatica, ArcGIS Pro, R, Fuzzy AHP

Precision Agriculture · Soil Science

Soil Nutrient & Suitability Mapping

Ikere, Oyo State

Multi-season spatial analysis of 11 soil parameters, including N, P, K, pH and organic carbon, combined into a land suitability assessment to guide crop planning at IAR&T.

Skills used: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, soil classification, spatial interpolation

Disaster Risk · Remote Sensing

Flood Extent & Vulnerability Mapping

Anambra West, Anambra State

Mapped flood extent for 2018, 2022 and 2024 from Sentinel-1 SAR on Google Earth Engine, then built flood vulnerability zones with AHP across nine factors: TWI, elevation, slope, drainage density, land use, soil, population density, distance to water and rainfall.

Skills used: Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-1 SAR, ArcGIS Pro, AHP, SRTM DEM
