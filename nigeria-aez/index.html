Agro-Ecological Zones of Nigeria

Tap anywhere to see the state, its share of the zone, and the other zones and states involved.

About this map

What it shows. Nigeria's agro-ecological zones, with every zone split by state. Tap any area to see how much of that state lies in the zone, which other zones the state contains, and every state the zone covers.

How the zones were made. A published 7-zone map was georeferenced and digitised. FAO GAEZ v4 length of growing period (1981–2010) was sampled along each boundary. Four boundaries follow it closely (about 97, 157, 218 and 283 days) and were redrawn from the growing-period contours. The Northern/Southern Guinea Savanna boundary does not follow growing period, so it is kept as published. Uplands (Jos Plateau; Mambilla and Obudu) come from the tropic-cool classes of the HarvestChoice AEZ layer. Lake Chad is mapped as open water, not as a zone.

How the numbers were calculated. Zones were intersected with state boundaries and areas measured in Africa Albers Equal Area. Percentages come from these spatial intersections, not from counts of LGAs.

Limits. Boundaries are generalised to a few kilometres and zones are gradients, not walls. Treat the map as regional, not field-scale.

Sources. FAO/IIASA GAEZ v4. Harvest Choice; International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI), 2015, "Agro-Ecological Zones for Africa South of the Sahara", doi.org/10.7910/DVN/M7XIUB, Harvard Dataverse, V3. Natural Earth. Coordinate system: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326).

Data. The shapefile and GeoPackage are available on request.
